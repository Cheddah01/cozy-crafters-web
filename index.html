What is this?

A cozy SMP with an actual memory.

We are not a giant network, and that is the point. Players build towns, run shops, make questionable decisions, and gradually turn the world into a place with history.

“Someone is probably starting political drama in The End.”

Why play here?

Simple survival, with more reasons to care.

🏡

Player towns

Build alone, join a town, or slowly become part of the server’s ongoing local politics.

🌾

Cozy economy

Trade with players, rent market space, use shops, and make money without turning survival into a chore.

🛡️

Protected builds

Claims keep your home safe while still leaving room for a shared world that feels alive.

✨

Custom polish

Custom menus, homes, ranks, markets, towns, goals, and small quality-of-life systems built for this server.
