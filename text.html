Best styles for Minecraft

Mini caps, fullwidth, and circled are usually the most useful for Minecraft menus. Fancy math/cursive/fraktur symbols can be inconsistent across clients, fonts, and resource packs.

Unicode styles are not real fonts. They are different characters, so some letters may not have perfect replacements.
