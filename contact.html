What to include

For a delivery, payment, username, or refund issue, provide:

  • the Minecraft Java username entered during checkout;
  • the approximate purchase date;
  • whether Supporter or Patron was purchased; and
  • a short description of what happened.

If available, a Stripe receipt or non-sensitive transaction reference can help locate the purchase.

Privacy questions

Email support@cozycrafters.net for privacy questions, requests to ask what personal information Cozy Crafters holds, and correction requests where appropriate. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Protect your payment information

Never send full card numbers, CVC security codes, passwords, Stripe secrets, or Minecraft account passwords. Cozy Crafters does not need those credentials to investigate a store purchase.
