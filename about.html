The server

More than a world that resets and gets forgotten.

Cozy Crafters is intentionally smaller than a giant network. Players can recognize one another, build towns with real identities, run shops, collaborate on projects, and leave a visible mark on the world.

The goal is familiar survival with thoughtful custom systems around it—not a maze of features competing for attention. Claims protect what you build, the economy gives projects a reason to grow, and quality-of-life additions make everyday play smoother.

“The best parts of a server are the stories players create together.”

Behind the server

Hey, I’m Cheddah.

I’m the owner and builder behind Cozy Crafters. I work on the server, its custom systems, the website, and the little details that make the whole experience feel connected.

For me, running the server is less about chasing the biggest player count and more about creating something thoughtful that people genuinely enjoy returning to. I like turning community ideas into real features, polishing the rough edges, and giving players tools to be creative.

Cozy Crafters keeps evolving because of the people who play here. Every town, screenshot, inside joke, suggestion, and strange chapter of server history helps shape what comes next.

If you would like to see more of my plugins and development work, you can explore everything over at Cheddah Development.
